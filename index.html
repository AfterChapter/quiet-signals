Listening for the Quiet Signals

A Self-Assessment for Anyone in Transition

Most transitions do not arrive without warning. They arrive in stages — first as a whisper you can almost ignore, then as something louder you can no longer pretend you did not hear. This assessment helps you identify where you are in that arc.

How to use this: Check every statement that feels true — even partially true. Your score updates at the top as you go. When you are done, find your stage in the results below.

Your Results

Look at your score above. Find the stage that matches your count below.

Stage 1  ·  1 to 5 signals

The Whisper

Something is asking for your attention — quietly. You may be tempted to explain it away. The fact that you are paying attention at all is more than most people allow themselves. Stay here. Keep listening.

Stage 2  ·  6 to 10 signals

The Nudge

The signals are becoming harder to dismiss. The transition may have already started — you just have not named it yet. This stage requires honesty more than it requires a plan. Give yourself permission to know what you know.

Stage 3  ·  11 to 14 signals

The Alarm

Multiple systems are telling you the same thing. This is not coincidence. The question is no longer whether something needs to change — it is what you are willing to do with that knowledge. You are not behind. You are awake.

Stage 4  ·  15 to 30 signals

Code Red  ·  You Knew It All Along

You did not miss the signs. You survived them. Like Dorothy, you had what you needed the whole time — you just had not been given permission to use it. What you are feeling is not weakness. It is the result of operating in an environment that was not built for you, for longer than was sustainable.
